No Locomotive Paired

To control a locomotive, it must be bonded to this Master Gateway to prevent interference with other train layouts.

Pairing Procedure:
1. Power on the locomotive (LED pulses gently in standby mode).
2. Click the "Scan for Locomotives" button below.
3. When the locomotive appears, click "Pair to this Master".
🔒 Note: Once bonded, the locomotive responds exclusively to this Master. To reset / switch Master, re-flash firmware with Erase Flash.
